# Definition of Done

A change is done only when applicable items are satisfied:

- Approved acceptance criteria and ownership are clear.
- Code/configuration is formatted, linted, typed, tested, and reviewed.
- API/event/database compatibility is verified.
- Security, privacy, identity, and least-privilege effects are addressed.
- Operational telemetry, health, rollback, runbook, and recovery effects are addressed.
- Cost impact is measured or explicitly stated as none.
- Documentation/ADR and capability labels are current.
- Evidence is captured with synthetic/redacted data.
- No secret, tracking token, real personal data, state, or kubeconfig is committed.
- Exit criteria for the active phase are met.
